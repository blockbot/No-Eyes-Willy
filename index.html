How to play

  • Willy can't see a thing, so he wanders on his own. He climbs ladders by himself.
  • Click or tap Willy to make him turn around.
  • Click or tap an empty box to fill it with a bridge piece (it stays filled) so Willy doesn't get impaled.
  • Get Willy safely to Martha to advance to the next level.
